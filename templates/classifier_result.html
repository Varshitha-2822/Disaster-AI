Immediate Safety Protocols

{% if label|lower == 'flood' %}
  • Move to higher ground; do not walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Do not touch electrical equipment if you are wet or standing in water.
  • Avoid contact with floodwater—it may be contaminated or hide hazards.
  • Stay tuned to local alerts and evacuation orders; follow official instructions.
{% elif label|lower == 'wildfire' %}
  • Stay indoors; close windows and doors; run AC on recirculation if smoke is heavy.
  • Wear N95 or P100 masks outdoors when air quality is poor.
  • Know your evacuation routes and meeting points; leave when advised.
{% elif label|lower == 'earthquake' %}
  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On—under a sturdy desk or table until shaking stops.
  • Stay away from windows, glass, and heavy objects that could fall.
  • If outdoors, move to an open area away from buildings, trees, and power lines.
{% elif label|lower == 'cyclone' %}
  • Stay indoors in a secure room away from windows; use lowest floor if multi-story.
  • Do not go outside until officials declare the storm has passed.
  • Avoid using candles if power is out; use battery lights.
{% else %}
  • Follow instructions from local emergency services.
  • Keep an emergency kit with essentials.
{% endif %}
